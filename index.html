About

I am a Colorado-based photographer, writer, and engineer who is completely obsessed with the outdoors.

Some days I'm working deep within low-level code to perform a security audit, others I'm miles off-trail in the middle of a photography backpacking adventure. Recently, I've been working on projects that are at the intersection of software and the outdoors.
